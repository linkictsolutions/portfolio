# MedyTic

- What it is / who it's for: E-medical app linking patients to caregivers.
- Platform: mobile
- Features: appointments; video calling (telehealth) + chat; connect wearable
  devices; upload medical files; auto-generated reports; medication history;
  shared family usage (shared events, shared appointments). Plans: Free,
  Individual, Family.
- The brief: (owner-defined product; Abel to interpret requirements and design
  the full UX/UI — expand from features as needed.)
- My role: End-to-end UX flows, UI, prototyping, design system. Worked with the
  front-end team.
- Key screens: family overview (hero / home), community hub (header & nav),
  family monitoring, invite friends, settings, appointment list + detail pop-up,
  add device. (100+ screens in full product.)
- Signature moment: appointment card → expands into detail sheet (primary);
  secondary later: wearable connect / family switcher if needed.
- Live prototype approach (no Figma embed required):
  rebuild hero screens as coded interactive UI; appointment expand is the
  touchable prototype. Full-flow Figma embed can be added later if a share
  link is provided — not blocking.
- Outcome: Shipped (~4 months ago).
- Year + status: 2026 / shipped.
- Accent color: #1987EE (from SVGs). Fonts: TBD from UI.
